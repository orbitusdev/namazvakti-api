# tr-places.json provenance

`tr-places.json` is generated by `scripts/build-places.mjs` (shape `[{ il, ilce, lat, lng }]`, 81 provinces, 973 districts).
No single open dataset provides both a current district list and district coordinates, so sources are combined.

## Sources

1. **Names and province coordinates** — [ubeydeozdmr/turkiye-api](https://github.com/ubeydeozdmr/turkiye-api),
   `datasets/provinces.json` and `datasets/districts.json` (2025 administrative divisions).
   License: MIT, Copyright (c) 2022-2026 Ubeyde Emir Özdemir.
   The central district of each non-metropolitan province is named `Merkez` in this dataset and is kept as is;
   metropolitan provinces use their real district names. `Merkez` gets the province coordinates.
2. **District coordinates** — [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/) (property P625, "coordinate location"),
   queried with `scripts/places.rq` against `https://query.wikidata.org/sparql`. License: CC0 1.0.

## Reproduce

```sh
git clone --depth 1 https://github.com/ubeydeozdmr/turkiye-api.git /tmp/turkiye-api
curl -sG https://query.wikidata.org/sparql --data-urlencode query@scripts/places.rq \
  -H 'Accept: application/sparql-results+json' -o /tmp/wikidata-districts.json
node scripts/build-places.mjs /tmp/turkiye-api/datasets /tmp/wikidata-districts.json
```

Districts are joined by diacritic-insensitive name. A Wikidata candidate must be filed (P131) under the
same province, and among several matches the one nearest to the province coordinates is used; a match
farther than 150 km from the province coordinates is rejected as wrong. Labels are normalized
(parentheses, `, Province`, `ilçesi`/`ilçe`/`district` suffixes removed, and `Merkez` suffixes retried).

3. **Overrides** — 53 districts have no usable Wikidata match (or lie beyond the 150 km cap, e.g. Anamur,
   Gazipaşa). Their coordinates are the label point of the district's administrative boundary in
   [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/) (ODbL, (c) OpenStreetMap contributors), listed in the
   `OVERRIDES` table in `scripts/build-places.mjs` with one relation URL per entry.

## Resulting coverage (973 districts)

| Origin | Count |
| --- | --- |
| Province centre (`Merkez`, by design) | 51 |
| Wikidata | 869 |
| OpenStreetMap overrides | 53 |
| Unresolved province-centre fallback | 0 |

## Known limitations

- No district falls back to its province's coordinates apart from the 51 `Merkez` districts, whose province
  centre is the intended point. A test fails if more than 10 districts share coordinates with a sibling.
- Coordinates are a representative point (Wikidata coordinate, or OSM boundary label point), not necessarily
  the municipal centre; for large districts it can be several km off the town. The practical accuracy impact
  is under about 1 minute of prayer time for a typical district.
- Wikidata and OSM points are not individually verified; the 150 km cap and province match only exclude gross errors.

# tr-diyanet-ids.json provenance

`tr-diyanet-ids.json` maps every district above (key `"il/ilce"`) to its Diyanet district id, used by
`EzanVaktiSource` to fetch Diyanet prayer times from the Ezan Vakti API (https://ezanvakti.emushaf.net,
an unofficial mirror of Diyanet's monthly tables). It is generated by `scripts/build-diyanet-ids.mjs`
with 82 requests (the province list and one district list per province), spaced 1.5 s apart and honouring
`429 Retry-After`; responses are kept in `$TMPDIR/ezanvakti-lists`, so reruns send no requests.

Each entry records how it was matched (`how`):

| How | Count | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `name` | 836 | Same name, diacritics and Diyanet's province letters (`AYVACIK (S)`) ignored; `Merkez` → the province's own entry |
| `alias` | 3 | Hand-checked spelling differences (`ALIASES` in the script), e.g. Doğubayazıt → `DOĞUBEYAZIT` |
| `nearest` | 134 | Diyanet has no entry of its own: the nearest Diyanet district of the same province, by our coordinates (`km`) |

Diyanet lists a metropolitan province's centre as one entry named after the province (`ANKARA`,
`İSTANBUL`), not its central districts; that entry is placed at the seat district in `PROVINCE_SEAT`,
so e.g. Çankaya, Kadıköy and Konak use their province's entry. The farthest `nearest` match is 28 km
(Derinkuyu → Nevşehir), worth about a minute of prayer time.
